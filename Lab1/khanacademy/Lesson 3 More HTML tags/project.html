Recipe #1: Chicken Ramen

  • Time: 30 minutes
  • Serves: 2
Ingredients Quantity
Ramen noodles 2 packs
Chicken breast 200 g
Water 1 litres
Soy sauce 2 tbsp
Egg 2

Step 1: Pour water into a pot and bring it to a boil. Add the ramen noodles and cook them for about 3–4 minutes until they become soft.

Step 2: Cut the chicken into small slices. Add the chicken to the pot and cook it together with the noodles for 5–7 minutes until the chicken is fully cooked.

Step 3: Add soy sauce to the soup and mix well. Serve the ramen in a bowl and place a boiled egg on top before eating.

Recipe #2: Vegetable Ramen

  • Time: 25 minutes
  • Serves: 2
Ingredients Quantity
Ramen noodles 2 packs
Carrot 1
Onion 1
Soy sauce 2 tbsp
Water 1 litres

Step 1: Pour water into a pot and bring it to a boil. Add the noodles and cook them for 3–4 minutes until they are soft.

Step 2: Cut the vegetables into small pieces. Add them to the pot and let them simmer with the noodles for about 5 minutes until the vegetables become tender.

Step 3: Add soy sauce to the soup and stir well. Serve the ramen hot in a bowl.
